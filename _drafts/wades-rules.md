I was doing some SciBowl sets a few days ago, and I came across something called "Wade's rules". I looked further into this, and it seems pretty cool. Wade's rules let us predict the shapes of borane clusters. Their shape depends on the number of boron atoms (obviously), along with the number of skeletal electron pairs (SEPs), which are just electron pairs that aren't part of a B-H bond.
