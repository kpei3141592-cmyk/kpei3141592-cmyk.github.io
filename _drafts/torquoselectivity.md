By the Woodward-Hoffmann rules, a $$4\pi$$ ring opening is conrotatory. However, for some asymmetrically substituted cyclobutenes, there is another type of selectivity that depends on which way the ring opens, called torquoselectivity. I originally thought this just progressed through sterics, i.e., the bulky group would rotate outwards, but it turns out that in 1984, the Houk group found some $$4\pi$$ openings that do not do this.
